Tabscape's code is free software under the [GNU General Public License v3.0 or later](LICENSE). You're welcome to use it, study it, change it and share it, on the GPL's terms.

The GPL covers the code, not the brand. Under section 7(e) of the GPL v3, no rights are granted to the **Tabscape name** or the **Tabscape logo** (`src/assets/logo.svg` and the icons made from it in `public/icons/`).

## What that means

- **Contributing to Tabscape:** no limits. Use the name and logo freely in this repository and in pull requests.
- **Talking about Tabscape:** fine. Write about it, link to it, review it, or say your project is "based on Tabscape".
- **Publishing your own version:** please give it a different name and logo, so nobody mistakes it for the original. Keep the GPL notices and credit, as the licence requires.

If you'd like to use the name or logo in some other way, ask on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/T5bF56tvyj).
